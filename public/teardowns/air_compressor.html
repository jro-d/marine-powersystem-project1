SOLAS Reg. II-1/26 & Two-Stage Polytropic Compression Proof

1. Optimum Interstage Pressure & Two-Stage Polytropic Work Saving

Compressing atmospheric air ($P_1 = 1.013\text{ bar abs}$, $T_1 = 308\text{ K}$) to $P_3 = 31.0\text{ bar abs}$ ($30.0\text{ barg}$) in a single stage would cause excessive discharge temperatures ($T_{out} > 295\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$, exceeding the $160\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ lube-oil coking/auto-ignition limit). Splitting compression across a $90^\circ$ V-twin with intercooling to $41\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ minimizes shaft work when stage pressure ratios are equal:

P_inter = √(P_1 · P_3) = √(1.013 × 31.013) = 5.60 bar abs (≈ 5.5 bar gauge)
T_2,LP = T_1 · (P_2 / P_1)^((n-1)/n) = 308 K × (5.53)^((1.30-1)/1.30) = 421 K (148 °C)
W_2stage = [2n / (n - 1)] · P_1 · V_1 · [(P_3 / P_1)^((n-1)/(2n)) - 1] (Saves 18.4% shaft power)

2. 90° V-Twin Slider-Crank Kinematics & Automatic Solenoid Unloader

Both connecting rods share a common forged crankpin ($R = 65\text{ mm}$, rod length $L = 220\text{ mm}$) while the LP cylinder bank is inclined at $\beta_{LP} = +45^\circ$ and the HP cylinder bank at $\beta_{HP} = -45^\circ$. During startup and standstill, the 24 V DC solenoid unloader vents interstage and aftercooler pressure so the 440 V squirrel-cage motor accelerates under zero back-pressure torque:

s_piston(θ, β) = R·cos(θ - β) + √(L² - R²·sin²(θ - β)) | CRANKPIN LOCK: ZERO DRIFT
Auto Pressure Switch: Cut-In @ 25.0 bar → Cut-Out @ 30.0 bar | Safety Relief Pop @ 33.0 bar
