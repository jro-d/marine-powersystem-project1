Mathematical Kinematic & Electrical Model Proof

1. Synchronous Speed & Grid Lockstep (IEC 60092)

For an 8-pole synchronous alternator supplying rated marine frequency f = 60 Hz:

N_s = (120 × f) / P = (120 × 60 Hz) / 8 poles = 900.0 RPM
Mechanical angular velocity ω_mech = 2π × (900 / 60) = 30π ≈ 94.248 rad/s

Alternator electrical phase angle: θ_elec = (P / 2) × θ_mech = 4 × θ_mech. Every single mechanical revolution (360°) produces exactly 4 AC electrical cycles (1440° electrical).

2. Slider-Crank Kinematics & Obliquity Ratio

Engine specifications: Bore B = 320 mm, Stroke S = 400 mm, Crank radius r = S/2 = 200 mm, Connecting rod length L = 720 mm.

Obliquity ratio λ = r / L = 200 mm / 720 mm = 5 / 18 ≈ 0.277778
Piston displacement: x(θ) = r × [1 - cos(θ) + (λ / 2) × sin²(θ)]
Conrod angularity: β(θ) = arcsin(λ × sin(θ))
