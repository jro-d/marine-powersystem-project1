1. Axial Fan Aerodynamic Affinity Laws & Reversible Airfoil Camber

For an 8-blade adjustable-pitch marine axial flow fan ($D_{tip} = 1,280\text{ mm}$, $D_{hub} = 480\text{ mm}$, annulus area $A_{ann} = \frac{\pi}{4}(D_{tip}^2 - D_{hub}^2) = 1.106\text{ m}^2$) serving a Category A Machinery Space, volumetric flow $Q$, static pressure rise $\Delta P_{stat}$, and shaft power $P_{shaft}$ obey the fan affinity laws:

2. Star-Delta ($Y\text{-}\Delta$) Starting Current Reduction & SOLAS Fire Damper

To prevent voltage dip on the 440 V main switchboard during high-inertia impeller acceleration, the local starter connects stator windings in Wye ($Y$) for the first $6\text{ s}$ ($V_{phase} = 440/\sqrt{3} = 254\text{ V}$), reducing starting current and torque to one-third ($1/3$) of Direct-On-Line ($I_{DOL}$). Under SOLAS Reg. II-2/5.2 & II-2/9, tripping the remote fire station or melting the $72\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ thermal fusible link vents the spring-return actuator, de-energizing the starter and snapping the 4 fire-red damper blades $90^\circ$ shut in $< 2.5\text{ s}$:

I_start,Y = (1 / 3) · I_DOL ≈ 2.0 × I_FLC = 145 A (vs 435 A DOL) → Transitions to Δ Run (72.4 A)
