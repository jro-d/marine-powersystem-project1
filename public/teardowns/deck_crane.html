1. 4-Part Hoist Reeving, Line Pull & 440 V HPU Power

For a Safe Working Load of SWL = 40 metric tons (392.4 kN) suspended on a 4-part wire-rope fall with sheave efficiency η_s = 0.96:

F_line = (SWL × g) / (n_falls × η_s) = 392.4 kN / (4 × 0.96) = 102.19 kN
P_HPU = (Δp × Q_pump) / (600 × η_total) = (280 bar × 540 L/min) / (600 × 0.90) = 280.0 kW

2. Luffing Cylinder Geometry & Overturning Moment

Twin double-acting hydraulic cylinders (`LC`) pivot between `[±0.32, 1.45, +0.35]` and the boom underside lugs. Outreach radius R(α) and pedestal overturning moment M_ped(α):

R(α) = R_pivot + L_boom × cos(α) (At α = 38°, R = 18.5 m)
M_ped = (m_cargo × g × R(α)) + (m_boom × g × 0.45 R(α))
