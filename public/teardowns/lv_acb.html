IEC 61439-2 / IEC 60947-2 Switchboard & De-Ion ACB Engineering Proof

1. 3200 A Double-Bar Copper Busbar Sizing & 65 kA Electrodynamic Force

Each phase utilizes twin parallel electrolytic copper bars (2 × 100 mm × 10 mm = 2,000 mm² per phase). At rated short-time withstand current I_cw = 65 kA rms (I_pk = 2.2 × 65 kA = 143 kA) and phase spacing d = 0.16 m:

F_peak/L = (μ_0 / 2π) × (I_pk² / d) = 2×10⁻⁷ × (143,000)² / 0.16 = 25.56 kN/m
S_3φ = √3 × 440 V × 3200 A = 2,438.7 kVA

2. De-Ion Arc Chute Quenching (18 Ferromagnetic Splitter Plates)

When the silver main fingers separate, current commutates onto the higher tungsten-silver arcing horns. Magnetic blow-out forces drive the arc into N = 18 steel splitter plates, creating N - 1 = 17 series cathode/anode voltage drops (V_an+cat ≈ 26 V per gap):

V_arc_total = (N - 1) × V_gap + E_col × l_arc ≈ 17 × 26 V + 180 V = 622 V > V_peak (440√2 = 622 V)

Because V_arc_total exceeds the 440 V system peak voltage, the arc cannot restrike after the first current zero (t_clear < 18 ms).
