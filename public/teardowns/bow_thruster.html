IEC 60092-501 & DNV Pt.4 Ch.5 Bow Thruster Kinematic Proof

1. Right-Angle Spiral Bevel Reduction & Hydraulic Pitch Servo Kinematics

The vertical 6.6 kV induction motor drives a 12-tooth spiral bevel pinion meshing at 90° with a 46-tooth propeller crown wheel inside the submerged pod, while an internal hydraulic push-pull rod actuates the 4 blade crank pins:

i_gear = Z_crown / Z_pinion = 46 / 12 = 3.8333 : 1 ⟹ n_prop = 1,150 / 3.8333 = 300.0 RPM
Servo Pushrod Stroke: s_servo(α) = R_pin · sin(α) = 85 mm × sin(α) (±35.92 mm at α = ±25°)

2. Hydrodynamic Thrust Force, Absorbed Power & Zero-Pitch Soft-Start

For a 4-blade Kaplan tunnel propeller (D = 1.80 m, ρ_sw = 1,025 kg/m³), lateral thrust and absorbed electrical power follow:

T(α, n) = K_T(α) · ρ_sw · n_prop² · D⁴ = ±168.0 kN (±17.13 tf) at α = ±25°, 300 RPM
P_elec(α, n) = P_idle(85 kW) + 1,115 kW · (|α| / 25°)^2.4 · (n_prop / 300)³
IEC 60092-501 Interlock: 6.6 kV breaker closing permitted ONLY when |α| ≤ 2.0° (Zero-Thrust Unloaded Start)
