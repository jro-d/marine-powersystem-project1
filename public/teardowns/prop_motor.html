IEC 60092-501 & DNV Pt.4 Ch.8 TEWAC Propulsion Motor Proof

1. Synchronous Electrical Frequency & Direct-Drive Shaft Torque

For a 12-pole slow-speed direct-drive marine propulsion motor ($p = 6$ pole pairs, $\theta_{elec} = 6 \cdot \theta_{mech}$) rated at $P_{mech} = 6,000\text{ kW}$ and $n_{rpm} = 145\text{ RPM}$:

f_elec = (p · |n_rpm|) / 60 = (6 × 145) / 60 = 14.50 Hz rated
τ_rated = (P_mech × 60) / (2π · n_rpm) = (6,000,000 × 60) / (2π × 145) = 395.1 kN·m

2. Propeller Load Law & Sea-State Fouling Factor ($k_{sea}$)

Propeller load torque scales with the square of shaft speed and sea-state margin $k_{sea} \in [0.80, 1.15]$, while mechanical shaft power scales cubically:

τ(n) = τ_rated · (n / 145)² · k_sea | P_mech(n) = τ(n) · (2π · n / 60)
I_stator = P_mech / (√3 · V_LL · cosφ · η_m) ≈ 665 A RMS at 5,600 V, 6.0 MW

3. TEWAC Double-Tube Closed-Loop Air-to-Water Heat Balance

Stator copper, iron core, and excitation losses ($P_{loss} \approx 180\text{ kW}$ at rated load) are extracted by the top-mounted CuNi 90/10 double-wall safety cooler into the ship's $32^\circ\text{C}$ LT freshwater circuit:

P_loss = 22 kW (no-load core/blower) + 158 kW · (I / I_rated)² = 180.0 kW
Hot Stator Exit Air: 68.0 °C → TEWAC Cooled Inlet Air: 40.0 °C (32.0 °C LT FW)
