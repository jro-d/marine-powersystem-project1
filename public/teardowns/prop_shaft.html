DNV Pt.4 Ch.4 & IACS UR M68 Propulsion Shafting & Stern Tube Proof

1. Hydrodynamic Propeller Thrust, Torque & Delivered Power

For the 5-blade high-skew Nickel-Aluminium-Bronze (`Cu3`) fixed-pitch propeller ($D = 2.40\text{ m}$, seawater density $\rho_{sw} = 1025\text{ kg/m}^3$):

T_thrust = K_T · ρ_sw · n² · D⁴ = 520.0 kN (53.0 tonnes) at 145 RPM
Q_torque = K_Q · ρ_sw · n² · D⁵ = 395.1 kN·m at 145 RPM (P_D = 2π·n·Q = 6,000 kW)

2. Michell Tilting-Pad Thrust Bearing & Hydrodynamic Oil Film

Eight kidney-shaped white-metal (Babbitt) tilting pads ($A_{pads} = 0.215\text{ m}^2$) support the integral $\varnothing 780\text{ mm}$ shaft collar via Reynolds hydrodynamic wedge lubrication:

p_pad = T_thrust / A_pads = 520,000 N / 0.215 m² = 2.42 MPa (24.2 bar < 3.5 MPa limit)
h_min ∝ √(μ · ω / p_pad) ≈ 62.0 µm at 145 RPM | SED Hull Potential < 50 mV

3. MARPOL Annex I Stern Tube Seal Differential Pressure Balance

The elevated gravity header tank & air-control unit maintain stern tube lube-oil pressure $0.30\text{ bar}$ above external seawater head so seawater can never ingress past the 4-ring Simplex lip seals:

P_sea = ρ_sw · g · h_draft / 10⁵ = 1.16 bar at 11.5 m draft
P_oil = P_sea + 0.30 bar = 1.46 bar (ΔP = +0.30 bar oil-over-sea margin)
