1. SOLAS Ch. II-1 Reg. 43 Automatic Bus-Tie & EDG Interlock Logic

Under normal sea/harbor conditions, the Emergency Switchboard (`ESB`) is supplied from the Main Switchboard (`MSB`) via the `440 V Bus-Tie ACB (52-BT)`. Upon loss of Main Bus voltage ($V_{MSB} < 0.85\,V_n$ for $> 3\text{ s}$), `52-BT` automatically trips open, the Emergency Diesel Generator (`EDG`) auto-starts, and `EDG Incomer ACB (52-EDG)` closes onto the dead ESB bus within $\le 45\text{ seconds}$:

Boolean Mechanical & Electrical Interlock: (52-BT CLOSED) ∧ (52-EDG CLOSED) = 0 (Unless Sync Check ANSI 25 Active)
Walking-Beam Pivot Angle: θ_beam = +14° (Normal: 52-BT ON) ↔ θ_beam = -14° (Blackout: 52-EDG ON)

2. 440 V / 220 V Dual-Bus Power Balance & Essential Feeder Sizing

The `440 V` emergency bus supplies heavy 3-phase motor loads (`Emergency Steering Gear`, `Emergency Fire Pump`, `Submersible Bilge Pump`) and feeds a `50 kVA, 440 V / 220 V Δ-Y` dry-type isolation transformer for `220 V` navigation (`Bridge ABT`), `GMDSS 24V Charger`, and `Emergency Lighting`:

I_ESB,440V = P_ESB / (√3 · 440 V · cosφ) = 184,000 W / (1.732 × 440 × 0.84) = 287.5 A (Busbar Rating: 1,200 A)
440V / 220V IT Earth-Free System: Dual Insulation Monitoring Devices (IMD) alarm at R_iso < 50 kΩ
