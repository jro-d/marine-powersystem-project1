1. 24-Pulse Phase-Shifted Rectifier & DC-Link Voltage

Four 6-pulse diode/thyristor bridges fed by phase-shifted converter transformer secondaries (-22.5°, -7.5°, +7.5°, +22.5°) cancel 5th, 7th, 11th, 13th, 17th, and 19th harmonics:

V_dc,rated = (3√2 / π) · V_LL = 1.3505 × 6,600 V = 8,913 V DC (V_dc,noload = √2 × 6,600 = 9,334 V)
Characteristic Harmonics: h = 24k ± 1 = 23rd, 25th, 47th, 49th (Line THD_I < 3.2%)

2. 3-Level Neutral-Point-Clamped (3L-NPC) PWM & Propeller Cube Law

Each inverter phase leg switches between +V_dc/2, 0 (NP), and -V_dc/2, producing a 5-level stepped line-to-line voltage waveform across the 6.0 MW propulsion motor:

Modulation Index m_a = f_out / 60.0 Hz ∈ [0, 1.0] | V_out,LL = m_a × 5,600 V RMS
Propeller Cube-Law Load: P_load(f_out) = 6,000 kW × (f_out / 60)³
Deionized Water Heat Rejection: Q_loss = 0.018 · P_load + 18.0 kW (σ_H2O < 0.50 µS/cm)
