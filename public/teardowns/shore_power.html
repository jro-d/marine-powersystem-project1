IEC/ISO/IEEE 80005-1 HVSC Kinematic & Interlock Proof

1. 6.6 kV / 7.5 MVA Cold-Ironing Ampacity & Slip-Ring Transfer

For a 3-phase 6.6 kV, 60 Hz High-Voltage Shore Connection rated at S = 7.5 MVA:

I_rated = S / (√3 × V_LL) = 7,500,000 VA / (1.73205 × 6,600 V) = 656.08 A
Cable Architecture: 3 × 185 mm² EPR + Concentric Cu Earth + 2 Pilot + 6 FO Cores

The two early-break pilot pins in the IEC 80005-1 plug (`CP`) are recessed by 14 mm relative to the L1/L2/L3 phase sockets so the 24 V DC safety loop opens before any high-voltage contact separation.

2. Constant-Tension Planetary Spooling Drive (`PD` & `RD`)

As the vessel rises and falls with tide and cargo draft, the 440 V VFD motor and planetary bevel reducer (`i = 84:1`) maintain constant cable tension F_t = 4.2 kN at pitch radius R(n):

M_drum = F_t × R_layer = 4,200 N × 0.494 m = 2,074.8 N·m
M_motor = M_drum / (i × η_gear) = 2,074.8 / (84 × 0.94) = 26.28 N·m
