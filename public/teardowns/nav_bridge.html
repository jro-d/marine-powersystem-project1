1. 12-Foot S-Band (3.05 GHz, λ = 9.84 cm) Slotted-Waveguide Array

S-Band (`3.05 GHz`) radar experiences minimal rain and sea-clutter attenuation compared to X-Band (`9.41 GHz`). A `12-foot` (`L = 3.66 m` full / `2.60 m` compact array) slotted-waveguide radiator achieves narrow horizontal beamwidth $\theta_H$ and high antenna gain $G$:

θ_H ≈ 70° · (λ / L) = 70° × (0.0984 m / 3.66 m) = 1.88° Horizontal, θ_V = 25° Vertical
Hits per Scan n_hits = (PRF · θ_H) / (6 · n_rpm) = (1,500 Hz × 1.88°) / (6 × 24 RPM) ≈ 19.6 pulses/target

2. SOLAS Reg. II-1/43 Automatic Bus Transfer (ABT) & COLREG Dual-Filament Monitoring

Navigation equipment and COLREG lights are fed by dual 220 V AC feeders (`Normal Bus B` and `Emergency Switchboard ESB`) via an Automatic Bus Transfer switch backed by a `0 ms` 24 V DC transitional UPS:

I_lamp = P_lamp / V_ac = 65 W / 220 V = 0.295 A per Navigation Lantern (5 Lights = 1.48 A)
If I_main < 0.08 A (Open Filament) → Auto-transfer to Standby Filament + Audible/Visual Alarm
