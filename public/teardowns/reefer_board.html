IEC 60092-509 & IEC 60309-5 Cargo Reefer Electrical Proof

1. Per-Container Reefer Load & VFD Harmonic Distortion (IEC 60092-509)

Each 40-foot marine refrigerated container operates on 440 V, 3-phase, 60 Hz via a 32 A 3P+E plug (`3h` ground clock position):

Pull-Down (-25°C): P_unit = 11.5 kW, cosφ = 0.84 ⟹ I_socket = 11,500 / (√3 · 440 · 0.84) = 18.0 A
Chilled Hold (+2°C): P_unit = 6.2 kW, cosφ = 0.84 ⟹ I_socket = 6,200 / (√3 · 440 · 0.84) = 9.7 A
VFD 5th & 7th Harmonics: I(t) = I_1 sin(ωt) + 0.12 I_1 sin(5ωt) + 0.08 I_1 sin(7ωt) (THD_I = 14.5%)

2. IEC 60309-5 Mechanical Interlock & 30 mA RCM Selective Coordination

To prevent open-arcing in wet cargo holds or on weather decks, each receptacle incorporates a mechanical cam interlock and a zero-sequence toroidal transformer:

Interlock Rule: (Switch = ON ⟹ Plug Locked) ∧ (Plug < 100% ⟹ Switch Locked OFF)
Zero-Sequence Sum: I_L1 + I_L2 + I_L3 = I_Δ | Trip if I_Δ ≥ 30 mA within t ≤ 28 ms
